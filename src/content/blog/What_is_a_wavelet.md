You know fourier transform right. It gives you the ability to see all the frequencies that exists in a signal. However, one of the main issue of this transform is that adding noise at a small section of the signal may affect the whole transform. This transform is not time localised. So to fix this issue,wavelets transform  have been created. They play the role of the cos and sin function that are perfectly localised in frequency space in the fourier transform are replaced by oscilating function that are both localised in time and frequency space. Due to heisenberg inequality we can't obtain both perfectly localised, but we can manage to get the energy of the function mostly around a certain point both in spectral and time space.
